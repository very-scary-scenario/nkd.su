support Neko Desu financially

None of us get paid for producing Neko Desu. In fact, Peter pays a membership fee yearly to be part of the radio station, as well as paying MixCloud for show storage and licensing, so any music bought for the show comes out of our own pockets. This is where you can help us.

We would like your help buying music for playing on the show. Any money contributed will be used primarily on digital music (such as from iTunes UK and Japan) or occasionally on CDs if available at a decent price (such as from second hand stores in Japan). We won't use the money raised for anything else. Donators have a say on what songs we buy too.

Please note: Donations of any kind will not make your votes on nkd.su worth more. Badges will not appear on the admin view Peter sees to remove any subconcious bias when selecting tracks to play.

You can support Neko Desu on a recurring basis on Patreon.

If you don't want to use Patreon for whatever reason, there are some other options:

  • send iTunes gift cards for iTunes UK or Japan to peter@nekodesu.radio
  • send money to Peter via Monzo or Starling.

Include your nkd.su username in your donation message to get the supporter badge. Include your Discord username to get access to the donator chat on the Neko Desu Discord.
